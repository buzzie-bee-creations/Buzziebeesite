How I Work

Everything on Buzzie Bee grows out of lived mental health experience, frontline care, and working alongside youth, Elders, and families across Vancouver Island.

  • Made for low-energy days Whether it's mini muffins, a blank safe foods sheet, or a pocket check-in tool, nothing here assumes you're starting with full batteries.
  • Rooted in real community care Shaped by years in crisis counselling, housing support, anti-violence work, and graduate study in Child, Youth, and Family Studies.
  • Honest, welcoming & accessible Grounded in 2SLGBTQ+ affirmation, practical support, and warm human connection without clinical jargon or pressure.
